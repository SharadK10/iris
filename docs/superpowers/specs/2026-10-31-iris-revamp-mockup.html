Every screen: iris branding, sunflower canvas

The geometric iris is now only the logo. Everything else is drawn from Iris's own hand-drawn botanicals: the translucent iris and sunflower illustrations already in the app. Brand marks are the iris only: the logo, the wax seal, the Pressed badge, the Now in Bloom mark. Sunflowers live in the canvas: the illustrations, the backgrounds, the flower tucked under the Bloom and under the seal, and the progress bar's playhead.
